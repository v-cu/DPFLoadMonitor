Settings

The options are grouped by topic – each one is described below.

Performance and data logging

Fast mode

Enables fast mode – the CAN bus is polled at a higher rate.

SD Card Save

Logs data to the SD card once per second. All parameters read on the main page are recorded.

Information display

Show Statistics

After the engine is switched off, the device shows the trip statistics for 10 seconds before going to sleep.

How to read the statistics

  • Red shows the maximum recorded values.
  • Blue shows the minimum recorded values.
  • For the calculated and measured soot mass, white shows the difference since engine start. For example, a value of +1.21 means the soot mass increased by 1.21 grams between starting the engine and the end of the trip. A value of -0.50 means the soot mass decreased by 0.50 grams.
Show Passive Regen

Signals passive regeneration on the main screen – the temperatures or the DPF icon turn orange. Detected from a drop in the measured soot mass.

Switch page to regen

During a DPF regeneration the screen switches automatically to page 5 – the one with the key DPF parameters.

Clear max after regen

After a regeneration finishes, the maximum values on page 7 (Differential Pressure Test) are reset automatically.

Audible signals

Buzzer

Audible signal when a DPF regeneration starts and finishes.

Buzzer Volume

Signal volume: HIGH – loud, LOW – quiet.

Screen brightness

Auto brightness

Switches between day and night mode automatically. With this option off, you switch modes manually by tapping the screen.

Day brightnessday mode

Screen brightness level in day mode.

Night brightnessnight mode

Screen brightness level in night mode.

Setting the clock

  1. Swipe up or down over the hours field to change the value.
  2. Repeat the same over the minutes field.
  3. Press the SET button to save.

DTC – fault codes

Fault codes are read automatically when you open the DTC page.

Read

Reads the DTCs (Diagnostic Trouble Codes) stored in the ECU memory. Use it to refresh the list manually.

Clear

Clears all DTCs stored in the ECU memory.

Regeneration history

  • CapacityThe last 10 DPF regenerations once full, the oldest entry is overwritten
  • OrderThe first record is always the most recent regeneration, the following ones are older
  • BrowsingSwipe left or right on the right-hand side of the screen
  • ClearingPress and hold the History button for 2 seconds

0-100 km/h acceleration test

  • Top rightCurrent measurement result
  • BelowThe last three results – the best one is marked green
  • Reset resultsPress and hold RESET to clear the last three results

  • RESETWaiting for the reset button to be pressed to start a new measurement
