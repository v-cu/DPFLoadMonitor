DPF Load Monitor

DPF Monitor is a compact device that shows the state of the diesel particulate filter in real time on VAG group vehicles. Plug it into the OBD2 socket and on the dedicated screen you immediately see the filter fill level, temperatures and regeneration progress.

Compatibility

CAN UDS
full functionality		4-digit engines Cxxx and Dxxx
VWTP2.0
limited version		1.9 TDI: BLS, BMM, BMN
VWTP2.0
limited version		2.0 TDI: CBAB, CBBB, CEGA

VWTP2.0 versions do not support DTC reading, microSD card logging or regeneration history.

Features

  • Readout of engine and DPF parameters
  • Audible and visual regeneration notifications
  • Logging data to microSD
  • History of the last 10 regenerations
  • Read and clear engine DTC codes
  • Quick differential pressure test
  • Acceleration measurement 0-100 km/h
  • Engine data charts
  • Automatic day/night mode
